Data Analyst skilled in Power BI, SQL & Advanced Excel | Operations & Supply Chain Analytics

I am a Data Analyst with an MSc in Logistics, Data Analytics & Supply Chain Management and hands-on experience developing end-to-end business intelligence solutions across healthcare, logistics and financial services. I specialise in using SQL, Power BI, Power Query, DAX and Advanced Excel to transform raw data into meaningful insights, build interactive dashboards, and support data-driven decision-making. My background in supply chain and operations management enables me to combine technical expertise with practical business knowledge to solve operational challenges and improve performance. I am open to Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Healthcare Data Analyst and Supply Chain Analyst roles, as well as freelance projects and consulting opportunities involving data cleaning, dashboard development, reporting and business analytics.
